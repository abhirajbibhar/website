<span class="caps">I study Anthropology,</span> a discipline that consistently demonstrate and scrutinize the diverse ways humans construct, interpret, and inhabit their social and cultural worlds. In other words Anthropology remind us that there are many ways to be Human.[^1]

This will be my portfolio cum blog site. It will collects my working notes, short and occasional long-form posts on how my life  and research going on.

Most weeks I am some combination of reading, writing, listen music, trying to explore python language I thought of learning three month ago, and sit to write the Introduction of my first paper for the seventh time. The [Writings](/posts) is where the semi filtered version of my thought process shows up.

[^1]: [Anthropology is the study of what makes us human.](https://americananthro.org/practice-teach/what-is-anthropology/)
