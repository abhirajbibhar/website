<span class="caps">I study Anthropology,</span> a discipline that consistently demonstrate and scrutinize the diverse ways humans construct, interpret, and inhabit their social and cultural worlds. In other words Anthropology remind us that there are many ways to be Human.[^1]

This site collects my working notes, and the occasional long-form post on how my research and personal life going on.

Most weeks I am some combination of reading, writing, listen music trying to explore a programming language I thought of learning past month, and rewriting the introduction of a paper for the sixth time. The [Blog](/notes/ignore-notes.md/) is where the semi filtered version of my thought process shows up.
